## The game in one line
A tiny firefly named Pip must hop through a pitch-dark mushroom forest, collecting spores to keep their light alive, before the Gloomshrooms snuff it out.

## The hook
Pip's small circle of light moving through total darkness, a spore bursting green. clips/02-cold-open-hop.mp4, first frame.

## The twist
The level is dark: you only see what your light reaches, and spores are both score and vision. Best shown in clips/03-spore-trail.mp4 (light grows with each spore) and paid off in clips/08-forest-glows-again.mp4 (the whole forest lights up).

## Look
- Palette: midnight blue #0b1026, mushroom purple #6b3fa0, spore green #7dff9a, firefly yellow #ffd84a, pale belly #fff3b0
- Type: chunky monospace/pixel feel, matching the game's title (bold Courier-like). Firefly yellow for the title, spore green for small lines.
- Transitions: hard cuts on the beat; dips to black; the light-circle motif as an iris transition (open on Pip, close to black) is welcome.
- Keep the game's pixels crisp: scale with nearest-neighbour, never blur.

## Sound
- Music: chiptune, slow and eerie at first (soft square-wave pad, minor key), building to a bright arpeggio at the payoff.
- Build: quiet open, rising through the swarm, silence on the black before the payoff, full hit when the forest glows.
- Game audio: clips are silent.
- Not: upbeat corporate/stock "business" music.
