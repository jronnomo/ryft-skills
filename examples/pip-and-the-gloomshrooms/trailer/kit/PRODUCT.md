# Product

## Register

game

## Users

Players browsing Ryft and social feeds for something fun to play right now in their browser. They decide in two seconds, from the gameplay alone, whether this looks fun. Especially cozy-spooky platformer fans.

## Product Purpose

Pip and the Gloomshrooms is a free 2D browser platformer on Ryft. A tiny firefly named Pip hops through a pitch-dark mushroom forest, collecting spores to keep their light alive before the Gloomshrooms snuff it out. The twist: you only see what your light reaches, so spores are both score and vision.

Success: someone watches the trailer, clicks "Play free on Ryft", and is playing within ten seconds.

## Brand Personality

Cozy, spooky, glowing. The trailer speaks in the game's voice, as if the forest were talking, never as a company.

## Anti-references

- Product launch videos: feature cards, claims, "streamline", "unlock", pricing energy
- Upbeat corporate stock music
- Generic gaming hype ("EPIC! INSANE!")
- Mock-ups or footage of things the game doesn't actually do
- Not a horror game: eerie and cute, never gory or jump-scary

## Design Principles

1. Gameplay is the star. Real footage from clips/, the majority of every cut.
2. The game's own look: palette #0b1026 #6b3fa0 #7dff9a #ffd84a, pixel art kept crisp.
3. Escalate. Each beat bigger than the last.
4. The twist (light = vision) gets the clearest shot.
5. End on the invitation: "Play free on Ryft".

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Text cards stay on screen long enough to read (about 0.3s per word). No rapid full-screen flashing (under 3 per second). Readable contrast against gameplay.
