# Product

## Register

game

## Users

Players browsing Ryft and social feeds for something fun to play right now in their browser. They decide in two seconds, from the gameplay alone, whether this looks fun. <Add who this game is especially for: cozy players, speedrunners, puzzle fans, kids...>

## Product Purpose

<Game Title> is a free 2D browser game on Ryft. <One-liner: the player must verb to goal while pressure.> <One sentence on the twist.>

Success: someone watches the trailer, clicks "Play free on Ryft", and is playing within ten seconds.

## Brand Personality

<Three words for the game's world and mood, e.g. cozy, spooky, glowing.> The trailer speaks in the game's voice, as if the world were talking, never as a company.

## Anti-references

- Product launch videos: feature cards, claims, "streamline", "unlock", pricing energy
- Upbeat corporate stock music
- Generic gaming hype ("EPIC! INSANE!") unless that is truly the game's voice
- Mock-ups or footage of things the game doesn't actually do
- <Anything off-tone for this game, e.g. "not a horror game, keep it cute">

## Design Principles

1. Gameplay is the star. Real footage from clips/, the majority of every cut.
2. The game's own look: palette <hexes>, its art style, its pixels kept crisp.
3. Escalate. Each beat bigger than the last.
4. The twist gets the clearest shot.
5. End on the invitation: "Play free on Ryft" + <link>.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Text cards stay on screen long enough to read (about 0.3s per word). No rapid full-screen flashing (keep any flash under 3 per second). Readable contrast against gameplay.
