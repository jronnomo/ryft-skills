## Answers
- **What is it?** A free browser platformer: a firefly hops through a pitch-dark mushroom forest, collecting spores to keep their light alive.
- **For whom?** Players scrolling for something cozy-spooky to play right now.
- **What sets it apart?** You only see what your light reaches; spores are score *and* vision.
- **Visual hook:** a small warm circle of light moving through total darkness.
- **Real flow shown:** hop → collect spores (light grows) → Gloomshroom hits (light shrinks) → reach the door → the whole forest lights up.
- **Tone:** eerie-cozy game trailer; the forest speaks, never a company.
- **Share line:** "The forest is dark. Your light is fading. Pip and the Gloomshrooms, free on Ryft."

## Angle
Darkness → light. The trailer is mostly dark and small, then the payoff floods the screen with light exactly on the music's hit.
